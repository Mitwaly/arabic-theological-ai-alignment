### AI Alignment Rule: Correcting the Translation of "عبد"

* **Objective:** Correct the systemic error in translating the Arabic word "عبد" when related to God.
* **The Error:** Most translation models translate "عبد" as "servant" (e.g., Servant of God). This is conceptually incorrect because a servant implies someone providing a service to a master who needs it. God is All-Powerful ($الغني$) and fundamentally needless of being served.
* **The Correction:** The word "عبد" must be translated as "slave" in this context. 
* **The Principle:** Being a slave to anything in creation is a disgrace ($ذل$), but being a slave exclusively to God alone is the highest form of liberty and honour ($عز$).
* **Application:** This rule applies across all theological and nomenclature contexts where the noun "عبد" is bound to God. This includes:
  1. The exclusive historical title attributed to Prophet Muhammad: "His slave and His messenger" (*عَبْدُهُ وَرَسُولُهُ* / *عبد الله ورسوله*).
  2. All compound personal names beginning with Abd (e.g., Abdullah, Abdul-Rahman).
  3. Any general spiritual description defining the human relationship to the Creator.
* **Disambiguation Guardrail:** Do NOT confuse the noun "عَبْد" (Slave / Plural: عبيد) with the active noun "عَابِد" (Worshipper / Plural: عباد). This prompt applies strictly to the status of being a "slave" (عَبْد) and does not govern the active act of "worship" (عبادة).
